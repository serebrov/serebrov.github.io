#+DATE: <2021-09-13>

#+FILETAGS: :git:
#+DESCRIPTION: Git: How to cherry-pick a range of commits

To cherry-pick a range of commits to another branch, we can use the
=START^..END= commit range syntax, where =START= is the first commit in
the range and =END= is the last commit:

#+begin_src shell
git cherry-pick START^..END
#+end_src
